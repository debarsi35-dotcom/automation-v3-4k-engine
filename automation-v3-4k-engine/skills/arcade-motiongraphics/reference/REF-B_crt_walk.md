## What to steal
1. **The HUD as a frame.** It just tells you "this is a game". In a story the HUD can carry meaning: health = story progress,
   coins = what the hero collects. Keep it small (y ≈ 110 px at 1080 width) and out of the way of the supers.
2. **The CRT glass** (scanlines + vignette + a diagonal reflection) for bookends: power on at the start, or pull back into a
   real cabinet at the end.
3. **Side-scroll parallax holds** for any "journey" beat: the hero walks in place and the world (history) scrolls past.
4. **Calm.** It is a loop, so nothing jumps. Use it as the breathing room between bursts.
