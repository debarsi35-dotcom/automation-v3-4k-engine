# Arcade Story Researcher & Fact-Checking Engine

Guides the research, verification, and scriptwriting process for 30–60 second Arcade Motion Graphics vertical shorts.

## Core Rules
1. **The 3-Act Arcade Game Arc**:
   - **Act 1 (Start / Level 1)**: A year + a tangible problem as the hook (0–2s). E.g., "1981. Nintendo is stuck with 2,000 arcade cabinets nobody wants."
   - **Act 2 (Struggle / Power-Ups)**: 2–3 concrete constraints solved through clever mechanics. "Can't draw hair? Give him a cap."
   - **Act 3 (Win / High Score)**: The identity reveal, big milestone number, and callback punchline.
2. **Dual-Source Verification**:
   - Every historical claim MUST be backed by 2 citations in `SOURCES.md` (e.g. SEC filings, founder memoirs, reputable news outlets, Wikipedia primary sources).
   - Omit contested myths (e.g. angry landlord demanding back rent).
3. **TTS Formatting**:
   - Use `_` to connect spoken words that represent one shown token: `"Two_thousand_five."` ↔ `"2005."`.
   - Provide phonetic hints for foreign names.
